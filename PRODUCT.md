# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective and current UQ Reality Labs members looking for club information, activities, projects, committee contacts and documents

## Product Purpose

The public website of UQ Reality Labs, the augmented and virtual reality student club at The University of Queensland in Brisbane

## Capabilities and Constraints

React with separate, directly addressable pages for About, events, projects, team, sponsors and yearly archives
Editable content belongs in public/assets/content as TOML, with Markdown and TOML frontmatter for documents
Generated application output belongs in dist
The original favicon.ico is authoritative
No invented official constitution, conduct policy, event, project, committee year or award
The user confirmed 2026 for the current committee and recovered general committee photographs

## Brand Commitments

The user selected a spatial journey through UQ St Lucia, Brisbane and Australia, applying Everest’s camera travel rather than adding Mount Everest
Igloo, Otherlife and Diamond Journey guide connected materials, lighting, object selection and reading context
Monocraft and Pixelify Sans lettering, pill controls, badges and pointer-responsive shadow
Place the UQRL logo between Reality and Labs, with logo-only brand, social and service links and readable main page navigation
The homepage browser title is exactly UQ Reality labs
The user rejected slogans, decorative arrows beside links, a visible pause control and the earlier cutaway room

## Evidence on Hand

Public committee portraits, sponsors, two awards, QuestRelay and approved document sources are retained in the content directory
OpenStreetMap footprints, Mapzen elevation, Translink route shapes and NASA Blue Marble imagery are credited in public/assets/world/SOURCES.md
Reference mechanisms and limitations are recorded in the external skills repository’s galleries ledger

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Real text and links precede optional 3D
Keyboard, touch, narrow screens, reduced motion, reduced data and failed WebGL must retain access to all content

## Open Decisions

Oracle connection target is unknown
Bot, election and service migrations remain separate planned applications
UQCloud porting is the last infrastructure migration
