UQ Reality Labs is a student club at the University of Queensland in Brisbane, Australia, bringing people together around augmented and virtual reality.

## Inside the lab

The club’s activities include workshops and demonstrations, with opportunities to meet other people interested in immersive technology.

## Join the club

[Membership is managed through Rubric](https://campus.hellorubric.com/?s=5809). [Meet the committee](/team/) or [find the club’s community links](/about/#contact).
