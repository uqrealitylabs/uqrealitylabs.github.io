## Watch together

QuestRelay shares Meta Quest video and supported game audio in a browser. The wearer starts sharing in the companion app and approves capture; viewers open a link.

## Made for playtests

Viewers can pin a feed, change the layout, mute audio and choose a theme. Administrators have a separate control room for access, headset controls and connection statistics.

## Current status

Two Quest 3 headsets have connected individually to the deployed relay. Simultaneous headset capacity, stable 60 fps and glass-to-glass latency still need verification. See the [project repository](https://github.com/uqrealitylabs/questrelay) for current capabilities and setup instructions.
